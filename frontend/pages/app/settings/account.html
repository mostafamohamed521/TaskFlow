Delete Your Account?

Deleting an account is permanent in the live product. It removes your profile and your access to every workspace. Workspaces you own must be transferred or deleted separately.

Account Ownership

Your account itself has no owner role. Ownership belongs to workspaces: you are Member of your workspace.

Transferring a workspace to another member will be available in Workspace Settings, which is part of the upcoming Workspaces module.
